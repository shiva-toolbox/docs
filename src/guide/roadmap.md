# Roadmap

What is already on the bot, and what comes next.

## Available now

- [Autorole](/guide/autorole), up to 10 roles, with a delay from immediate to one hour.
- [Join and leave messages](/guide/join-leave), with your own title and text.
- [Twitch live alerts](/guide/twitch), up to 10 channels.
- [Language](/guide/language), English or Portuguese, for this server.

## Next

- YouTube live and video alerts
- Kick alerts
- Reaction roles
- Custom commands
- Interactive dashboard
- Moderation with punishments
- Event log and audit
- Member counter
